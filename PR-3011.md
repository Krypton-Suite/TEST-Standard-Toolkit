# Fix: Cannot drag maximized form from the very top (Issue #3011)

## Summary

Fixes a regression where it is not possible to drag a maximized `KryptonForm` from the very top of the screen. Users had to move the mouse cursor down one or two pixels before dragging would work. Double-click to restore also failed when performed at the top edge.

## Related Issues

- **Closes** [#3011](https://github.com/Krypton-Suite/Standard-Toolkit/issues/3011) – [Regression] Cannot drag maximized form from the top (once again)
- **Regression of** [#2096](https://github.com/Krypton-Suite/Standard-Toolkit/issues/2096) – Cannot drag maximized form from the top after upgrading to V90

## Root Cause

In `WindowChromeHitTest`, when the cursor is at the very top of a maximized form (y ≤ 8 pixels), the hit test was returning resize handle codes:

- `HTTOP` – top resize border
- `HTTOPLEFT` – top-left corner
- `HTTOPRIGHT` – top-right corner

For a maximized window, these codes do not initiate a drag. Windows expects `HTCAPTION` in the title/caption area to allow dragging. Returning resize handles instead caused the top edge to be non-draggable.

## Solution

Added an early check in `WindowChromeHitTest` inside the border hit-test block: when the form is maximized and the point lies in the top area (`pt.Y <= Math.Max(borders.Top, HT_CORNER)`), return `HTCAPTION` instead of `HTTOP`, `HTTOPLEFT`, or `HTTOPRIGHT`.

## Files Changed

- `Source/Krypton Components/Krypton.Toolkit/Controls Toolkit/KryptonForm.cs`

## Testing

1. Create or open a `KryptonForm`.
2. Maximize the form.
3. Move the mouse to the very top of the screen (top edge of the form).
4. **Verify:** Click and drag—the form should move/restore as expected.
5. **Verify:** Double-click the top edge—the form should restore to its previous size.
